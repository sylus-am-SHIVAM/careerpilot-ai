# Business & Management Careers

## Product Manager
Product managers decide what to build and why, working between users, engineers, designers and business teams.
Core skills: user empathy, prioritisation, communication, data analysis (SQL, metrics), writing product requirement documents, basic technical understanding.
Learning path: learn product thinking (problem first, then solution), analyse and write case studies on real products, work on a product with a team (college club, startup, side project), learn basic SQL and analytics.
Entry routes in India: Associate Product Manager (APM) programmes, MBA, or moving from engineering, design or analytics roles into product.
Portfolio ideas: product teardown documents, a feature proposal with metrics, a side project you launched.

## Business Analyst
Business analysts study business processes and data to recommend improvements.
Core skills: Excel, SQL, data visualisation, requirement gathering, process mapping, communication.
Learning path: spreadsheets and SQL, then dashboards, then business case studies, then internships in operations, consulting or analytics teams.
Portfolio ideas: an analysis of a company's public data with recommendations, a process-improvement case study.

## Entrepreneur
Entrepreneurs start and grow new ventures that solve real problems.
Core skills: identifying problems, customer discovery, building a minimum viable product, sales, financial basics, resilience, team building.
Learning path: talk to potential customers, build small experiments, learn basic finance and marketing, join a startup to learn, use college incubation cells and entrepreneurship cells (E-Cells).
India support: Startup India, college incubators and university innovation programmes offer mentoring and support.
Free resources: Y Combinator Startup School, Paul Graham's essays.

## Management Consultant
Consultants help organisations solve business problems and improve performance.
Core skills: structured problem solving, case analysis, Excel and presentation skills, communication, business acumen.
Learning path: practise case interviews, join consulting clubs, do internships, build strong academics.
Entry routes in India: top MBA programmes (admissions through CAT and other exams) and selective undergraduate campus placements.
