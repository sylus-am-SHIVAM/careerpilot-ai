# Creative Arts & Design Careers

## UI/UX Designer
UI/UX designers research user needs and design intuitive, attractive digital products.
Core skills: user research, wireframing, prototyping, visual design principles, typography, colour, usability testing, tools such as Figma.
Learning path: learn design fundamentals, then Figma, then redesign existing apps with clear reasoning, then do real projects with user feedback, then build a case-study portfolio.
Entry routes in India: B.Des programmes (entrance exams include UCEED and NID DAT), or any degree plus a strong portfolio. Many designers enter through internships at startups.
Free resources: Google UX design content on YouTube, Figma's own tutorials, Laws of UX, Nielsen Norman Group articles.
Portfolio ideas: 3 detailed case studies showing problem, research, design iterations and outcome.

## Content Writer
Content writers create articles, scripts, social media posts and marketing copy.
Core skills: clear writing, research, storytelling, editing, SEO basics, adapting tone for audiences.
Learning path: write consistently (blog or newsletter), learn SEO and content marketing basics, take freelance or internship assignments, specialise in a niche such as tech, finance or education.
Portfolio ideas: a personal blog, published articles, sample marketing copy, case studies of traffic growth.

## Graphic Designer
Graphic designers communicate ideas visually through branding, illustrations, posters and digital media.
Core skills: design principles, typography, colour theory, layout, tools such as Adobe Illustrator/Photoshop, Figma or Canva, branding.
Learning path: fundamentals of design, daily practice, recreate professional designs, then client or freelance work, then build a focused portfolio.
Free resources: Canva Design School, YouTube design channels, Behance and Dribbble for inspiration.

## Film and Media Producer
Media professionals create films, videos, podcasts and digital content.
Core skills: storytelling, scriptwriting, shooting and editing video (for example DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro), sound, project management.
Learning path: create short videos regularly, learn editing, collaborate on student films, study film through courses or a degree, then specialise (direction, editing, production, cinematography).
Entry routes in India: film and media institutes such as FTII and SRFTI, mass communication degrees, or building a strong online channel portfolio.
