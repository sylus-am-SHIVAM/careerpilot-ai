# Science & Research Careers

## Research Scientist
Research scientists investigate open questions through experiments, analysis and publications, in universities, government labs or company R&D teams.
Core skills: deep subject knowledge, research methodology, statistics, scientific writing, reading papers, programming for analysis (Python or R).
Learning path: build strong fundamentals in your subject, join a professor's project or a summer research internship, learn to read and summarise papers, then aim for a master's or PhD.
Entry routes in India: B.Sc/B.Tech followed by M.Sc/M.Tech/PhD. Common exams and programmes include JAM (for M.Sc at IITs), GATE, CSIR-NET/UGC-NET, and institutes such as IISc, IISERs and IITs. Summer research fellowships (for example the Indian Academy of Sciences fellowship) are a good first step.
Free resources: NPTEL, MIT OpenCourseWare, Google Scholar, arXiv.
Portfolio ideas: a research internship report, a reproduced result from a published paper, a poster at a student conference.

## Data Analyst
Data analysts clean, analyse and visualise data to answer business or research questions.
Core skills: Excel or Google Sheets, SQL, Python or R, statistics, dashboards (Power BI, Tableau or Looker Studio), clear storytelling with data.
Learning path: spreadsheets and SQL first, then statistics, then a visualisation tool, then Python for analysis, then case-study projects.
Free resources: Google Data Analytics content on YouTube, Kaggle Learn, Mode SQL tutorial, freeCodeCamp data analysis course.
Portfolio ideas: an end-to-end analysis of a public dataset with a dashboard and written recommendations.

## Biotechnologist
Biotechnologists apply biology and technology to develop products in healthcare, agriculture and industry.
Core skills: molecular biology, lab techniques, biochemistry, bioinformatics basics, data analysis, documentation and lab safety.
Learning path: strong biology and chemistry fundamentals, a degree in biotechnology or life sciences, lab internships, then specialisation (for example bioinformatics, genetics or bioprocess engineering).
Entry routes in India: B.Tech/B.Sc Biotechnology, then M.Sc/M.Tech. Exams such as GAT-B and JAM are used for postgraduate admissions.
Free resources: NPTEL biotechnology courses, Khan Academy biology, NCBI tutorials for bioinformatics.

## Professor and Academic Researcher
Academics teach at colleges and universities while doing research and mentoring students.
Core skills: subject expertise, teaching and communication, research and publishing, grant writing, mentoring.
Learning path: excel in your subject, gain research experience early, complete a master's and PhD, publish papers, and gain teaching experience as a teaching assistant.
Entry routes in India: UGC-NET or CSIR-NET qualification and/or a PhD are typically required for assistant professor roles.
